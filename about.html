About the project

CaesiumOS as a whole began on October 25, 2021.

That date is when the jpbROM project originally commenced. It would go through a number of renames, such as hueOS (2024-25) and CaesiumOS (2025).

The name CaesiumOS (not to be confused with an earlier project named Cesium OS, which was deprecated around 2020/21) initially appeared on July 12, 2025 as a rebrand of what was then known as hueOS.

At the time, there was a parallel project (also developed mainly by jpb) named MantissaOS. The initial name of this project was not MantissaOS, however. In fact, it was named Lilac UI when experimentation with making a LineageOS fork began.

This means that what is now known as CaesiumOS (Conception) had its own series of rebrands...

Lilac UI (2024-25) -> MantissaOS (2025) -> CaesiumOS "L"/pre-Conception (2025-26) -> CaesiumOS Conception (2026) -> CaesiumOS (Conception) (2026-present)

The latest series of renames/rebrands were conducted in August 2026.

For anyone curious when rebrands were conducted or new Android-based projects launched:

October 2021 - launch
July 2024 - launch and rebrand
February 2025 - rebrand
July 2025 - rebrand
October 2025 - rebrand
April 2026 - rebrand
June 2026 - rebrand
July 2026 - launch
August 2026 - rebrand/restructurization

Yes, seriously. That is a ton of rebrands. I guess it just shows that for two years (July 2024-August 2026), after expanding its scope massively, there was a lot of indecision about where it would head or what it would be for. Ultimately, in August 2026, the aims were set, along with a few finalizing rebrands) and hopefully won't be changed in a while!
